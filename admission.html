Instructions

College offers admission for various Under-Graduate, Post-Graduate and Diploma programmes. Candidates are required to fill the application form and submit it with the following:

  • Four passport size coloured photographs
  • Photocopy of mark-sheet and certificate of all previous courses
  • Original character certificate and transfer certificate from the last school/college attended
  • Affidavit in case of any gap between year of previous course and current admission
  • Copy of caste certificate

Admission is based on merit and personal counselling. Candidates must bring original documents for verification.
