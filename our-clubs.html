Rejoice... Club for Sports Activities

(A Unit of Maharaja Agrasen Mahavidyalaya, Bareilly)

About MAM

Maharaja Agrasen Mahavidyalaya, an institute of Maharaja Agrasen Shiksha Samiti (Regd.), Bareilly, has been scaling new heights in education and sports for 26 years. The Mahavidyalaya has also run the Shri Agrasen Cricket Academy and is now setting up the "Rejoice... Sports Club" on its campus. The club aims to promote health, social cohesion, and community engagement through structured sports activities. It also focuses on providing opportunities for skill development, stress relief, and fostering a sense of belonging.

Aim

The primary objective of this sports club is to offer organized sports activities to promote physical and mental well-being. It seeks to encourage social interaction, skill development, sportsmanship, and both competitive and recreational participation.

Objective of the Club

The “Rejoice... Sports Club” aims to revive and promote traditional Indian games such as gendtadee, sapolia, kabaddi, tug of war, and kho-kho—games that are being forgotten in today's digital era. The club focuses on encouraging physical and mental health among children and youth by explaining the harmful effects of online games and fostering real-world physical activities. These traditional and modern sports will help build social harmony and a stronger community.

Sports Club's Rules & Regulations

  1. Carry your Institute Identity Card at all times.
  2. Sign the register before entering and leaving the indoor complex.
  3. Follow instructions issued by RSC from time to time.
  4. Maintain silence in the Indoor Hall.
  5. No food, gum, or drinks (except water) during activities.
  6. No inappropriate behavior or language.
  7. Stack equipment neatly after use. Report breakages.
  8. RSC is not responsible for personal item loss or theft.
  9. Turn off lights/fans after play. Do not tamper with electrical items.
  10. Tobacco, pan, gutkha, and other banned items are strictly prohibited.

Cultural Club - Tarangini

Mentor: Dr. Sonal Agarwal

The Cultural Club “Tarangini” aims to foster cultural awareness and creativity among students by organizing various festivals, competitions, and traditional events to preserve and celebrate Indian heritage.

Health Club - Aarogya

Mentor: Dr. A.K. Sharma

“Aarogya” promotes healthy lifestyles through workshops, yoga sessions, and awareness programs focused on physical and mental well-being of students and faculty.

Language Club - Global Tongues

Mentor: Dr. Seema Shrivastav

This club promotes linguistic skills through debates, extempore speeches, essay writing, and language learning sessions, helping students gain global communication competence.

Talent Club - Creative Canvas

Mentor: Dr. Bipul Mehrotra

“Creative Canvas” offers a platform to showcase talents in arts, music, drama, and crafts. It nurtures artistic expression and innovation among students.

Environment Club - Clean & Green

Mentor: Dr. Nazo Bi

This club raises environmental consciousness through campaigns, tree planting drives, cleanliness initiatives, and sustainability workshops.

Social Club - Sajag

Mentor: Mr. Shivam Gangwar

“Sajag” is devoted to community service and awareness drives. It organizes social welfare activities like blood donation camps, street plays, and awareness rallies.

Academic Excellence Club - Medha

Mentor: Dr. Arti Bansal

“Medha” promotes academic brilliance through quizzes, seminars, and research-based activities, motivating students to achieve excellence in studies and knowledge.
