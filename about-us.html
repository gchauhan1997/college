About MAM

Maharaja Agrasen Shiksha Samiti (Regd.)

Maharaja Agrasen Shiksha Samiti (Regd.), Bareilly is a charitable trust of socially dedicated trustees. On 4th February, 1995 Maharaja Agrasen Shiksha Samiti laid down the foundation stone of Maharaja Agrasen Mahavidyalaya at 11th k.m. milestone on Bareilly- Lucknow National Highway (NH-24). Maharaja Agrasen Mahavidyalaya started imparting education in 1998 with students of B.Sc. classes. After the continuous efforts of the faculty, staff and able guidance of members of Maharaja Agrasen Shiksha Samiti, College has evolved as one of the premier centre of higher education in Bareilly city, embracing more than 2200 students getting education in science, Arts, Commerce, Education, Computers and Management streams etc in 21 Courses. MAM also got the Recognition from AICTE for BBA and BCA Courses. We are also trying to impart computer accounting education to every student. We also Started Shri Agrasen Cricket Academy and Idea Café, Innovation Centre & NATS under the State and Central Government Scheme.

Our Campus Rules

Discipline

The College pursues the ideals of training of minds and quest for knowledge and of healthy life. It upholds its traditions of free inquiry and tolerance. It lays great emphasis on mental and physical discipline and expects the students to strictly abide by the rules of discipline in and outside the classroom.

Attendance

75% attendance is mandatory for all students. If a student falls short of the compulsory attendance in college, he/she will automatically be debarred from the examination as per university guidelines.

Anti-Ragging Committee

Ragging is criminal offence as per the Honorable Supreme Court verdict. Ragging in any form is legally banned. Our Mahavidyalaya campus is totally ragging free. It has a vigilant anti ragging committee which is constitute to prevent ragging.

Women Grievance Cell

This is meant to safeguard and promote well-being of all women employees of the college. It cares for all complaints & requirements of women staff and action is taken for redressal of complaints through internal complaint committee.

Fee Rules

Fee once deposited is not refundable in any circumstances. This applies to completion of course, rustication as well as withdrawal by students from Mahavidyalaya. All disputes are subject to jurisdiction of the court in Bareilly. Student has to deposit the enrolment fee and University examination fee separately.

Dress Code

There is a uniform code for all students which ensures uniformity among students.
Note: The Shirt, Kurta, Sweater and Blazers should have the monogram of the college.

Cell Phones

Cell phones are strictly banned in the college campus.
